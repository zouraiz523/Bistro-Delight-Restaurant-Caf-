Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Breakfast is served until 11:30 AM, lunch from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM, and dinner from 5:00 PM to 9:30 PM.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes, we have a variety of vegetarian and vegan options available on our menu. Please inform your server about any dietary requirements.

Is outdoor seating available?

Yes, we have a beautiful patio for outdoor dining. Outdoor seating is available on a first-come, first-served basis, but you can request it when making a reservation.

Do you accommodate large groups?

We can accommodate groups of up to 20 people. For larger groups, please contact us directly to discuss special arrangements.

Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Yes, we offer both takeout and delivery services. You can place an order by calling us or through our partner delivery apps.
